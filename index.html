Neighbor Spotlight: The Martinson Family

When Kevin and Amy Martinson moved to Maplewood Commons three years ago, their front yard was standard fescue. Today it's a thriving, ACC-approved pollinator paradise featuring native Georgia wildflowers, milkweed for monarchs, and a small water feature that's become a neighborhood landmark. "We just wanted to do something good for the bees," Amy shared. Three other households have since submitted ACC requests for similar gardens. The board fully encourages these beautiful, sustainable upgrades!

Spring Lawn & Home Care Checklist
✅ Aerate & overseed fescue lawn in early spring
✅ Clean gutters of winter debris before rain season
✅ Inspect irrigation heads before activating systems
✅ Pressure wash driveways and sidewalks
✅ Mulch beds with 3" of pine straw or hardwood
✅ Check HVAC filters before cooling season begins

Official records of all HOA board meetings — transparent governance for our community.
February 25, 2026 — Regular Board Meeting ✅ Approved · 5 of 5 Present
Call to Order

President Robert Hayes called the meeting to order at 7:04 PM. A quorum was confirmed with all five board members present. Sandra Mills serving as Secretary.

2026 Budget Approval

Treasurer Tom Kwon presented the proposed 2026 operating budget of $142,800. Reserve fund contributions remain at $28,500/year. Motion to approve: Hayes. Second: Ross. Approved 5-0. Quarterly dues unchanged at $385.

Pool Inspection Report

David James reported results of annual pool inspection. Filtration system requires replacement. Three bids reviewed: AquaClear ($4,200), PoolPro ($5,100), Blue Wave ($4,850). Motion to accept AquaClear bid: James. Second: Mills. Approved 4-1.

ACC Application — 14 Birchwood Ct

Application for 6' cedar privacy fence replacement reviewed. Meets community standards. Motion to approve: Mills. Second: Kwon. Approved 5-0.

Adjournment

Meeting adjourned at 8:32 PM. Next meeting: March 18, 2026 at 7:00 PM, Community Clubhouse.

January 21, 2026 — Regular Board Meeting ✅ Approved · 4 of 5 Present
2026 New Year Priorities

Board discussed priority projects: community portal launch, entrance monument restoration, and pool area improvements. Three committees formed with volunteer leads.

Community Portal Launch

Portal presented to board. Unanimously praised for design and functionality. Board approved soft launch to residents by February 1st.

Entrance Monument Project

GreenScape Atlanta selected at $3,600 — $400 under approved budget. Work begins January 27th.
